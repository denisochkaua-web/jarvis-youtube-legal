Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

JARVIS is a personal tool. It connects only to its owner's own YouTube channel through Google OAuth.

What it accesses

  • YouTube channel information, the list of uploaded videos and their statistics (read-only).
  • Uploading new videos and changing the privacy setting of the owner's own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel (read-only).

What it does not do

  • It does not access other people's accounts or data.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use the data for advertising.

Storage

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer, in an encrypted password store. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

JARVIS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
